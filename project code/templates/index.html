Problem

The Challenge of Weeds in Agriculture

Weed is an unwanted plant in agricultural fields that aggressively consumes nutrients, water, sunlight, and soil vitality intended for sesame crops. This results in reduced crop yield and financial loss for farmers.

Conventional farming relies on blanket pesticide spraying across the entire field. This leads to massive chemical waste, high costs, toxic runoff into soil, and pesticide residues clinging to crops which pose severe health risks to human consumers.

Aim

Our AI Precision Solution

We aim to develop a smart AI vision system that detects precise bounding box locations of weeds and crops, enabling robotic sprayers to trigger micro-jets only on weeds while bypassing crops.

  • Reduces pesticide wastage by 70% – 85%
  • Eliminates chemical contamination on human food crops
  • Protects soil microbiome and water tables
